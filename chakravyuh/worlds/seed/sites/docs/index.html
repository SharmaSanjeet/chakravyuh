Auralis Docs

Engineering handbook for the Auralis observability platform.

Auralis ingests metrics, traces and logs from customer fleets, normalises them into a single time-series store, and serves dashboards and alerts on top. These pages document how the platform is operated internally — on-call engineers and platform teams are the primary audience. Access requests are handled through the operations console.

How the platform fits together

Telemetry enters through ingest-gateway, is reduced by the stream-processor tier, and lands in columnar storage where the query-engine serves reads. Routing of alert deliveries is delegated to alert-router; expiry of raw spans is owned by retention-worker.

Operating conventions

Every service publishes a health document and ships structured JSON logs to the central collector. Releases roll out through the deployment pipeline with automatic rollback on failed canaries. Start with Getting started to wire up a local environment.
